A little more clarity

Before you begin.

What kind of resume can I upload?

A PDF under 6 MB with selectable text, up to 30 pages. Scanned images need OCR first. If you already have the project’s parsed resume JSON, you can load that in the matching workspace.

What happens after I upload?

Your PDF is read and its explicit skills and qualifications are mapped into a profile. You’ll move to the matching workspace to review those facts, explore roles, and draft outreach. Some details may remain “not documented,” so check your extracted profile before relying on it.

Are these live job listings?

The default demo includes eight synthetic job postings. Your team can load its own postings in the workspace or start the server with its job dataset. The catalog label tells you which source is being used.

Where does my resume go?

The default setup processes your PDF on the server running this app without calling an AI service. The PDF is removed after parsing; extracted facts stay in your session until it expires or the server restarts. If your team enables the optional Voyager features, resume facts may be sent to that configured service.
